# Roller Sports Association Moradabad (RSAM)
## Official Web Portal — End-User Guide

Welcome to the official web portal of the **Roller Sports Association Moradabad (RSAM)**, affiliated with the Uttar Pradesh Roller Skating Association (UPRSA) and recognized by the Roller Skating Federation of India (RSFI).

This guide explains how athletes, parents, coaches, and sports enthusiasts can navigate and utilize all features of the RSAM portal.

---

### 1. Website Navigation & Sections

The RSAM website is designed as a fast, single-page web application (SPA) with dedicated sections:

- **Hero Banner (`#hero`)**: Highlighting RSAM Moradabad announcements with quick links to Register Skater and Explore Photo Gallery.
- **About RSAM (`#about`)**: Association history, district mission, and affiliation credentials with UPRSA & RSFI.
- **Officials (`#officials`)**: Executive body members, certified coaches, and district leadership directory.
- **News & Announcements (`#news`)**: Latest press releases, championship trial notices, and official circulars.
- **Highlights (`#highlights`)**: Spotlighting national & state medalist athletes (e.g. Vedanga Sharma, RSFI Nationals).
- **Photo Gallery (`#gallery`)**: Live Cloudinary photo showcase categorized by event albums.
- **Official Recognition & Skinsuit Showcase (`#certificate`)**: Accreditation certificates, PAN card, and the official RSAM Skater Racing Skinsuit uniform showcase.
- **Contact / Connect (`#connect`)**: Floating contact drawer to submit inquiries directly to RSAM officials.

---

### 2. Photo Gallery & Event Showcase

The **Event Showcase Gallery** dynamically fetches championship photographs directly from RSAM's Cloudinary cloud media storage.

#### Viewing Event Albums
1. Scroll to the **Photo Gallery** section (`#gallery`).
2. Each event card displays:
   - Event Title, Date, and Location.
   - Total number of live photos present in the Cloudinary folder (e.g. `📷 146 Photos`).
   - Category badge (e.g., *State Championship*, *Felicitation*).
3. Click **📂 Open Event Folder** on any card to view photos inside that album.

#### Gallery View Modes & Pagination
- **Grid View / List View**: Switch between grid thumbnail view and detailed list view using the toggle buttons at the top right.
- **Pagination**: Photos are displayed **20 items per page**. Use the `‹ Prev`, `Next ›`, or page number buttons at the bottom to browse thumbnails.
- **Fullscreen Lightbox Showcase**: Click any photo thumbnail to open the High-Quality Lightbox Showcase.
  - View uncompressed master images.
  - Navigate using keyboard `Left Arrow (←)` / `Right Arrow (→)` or `Escape` to close.
  - Click any thumbnail in the bottom strip to jump directly to a photo.
- **Empty Albums**: If an event folder has `0 photos` uploaded on Cloudinary, an empty folder status notice is displayed.
- **Returning to Folder Overview**: Click **`← Back to All Event Folders`** at the top left of the album view to return to the folder grid.

### 3. Annual Skater Registration 2026 (`register.html`)

All district skaters participating in RSAM coaching camps, district trials, and UPRSA state selections must complete annual registration.

#### Step-by-Step Registration Process:
1. Click **`🛼 Register Skater`** on the main navigation or visit [`register.html`](file:///d:/rsam-website/register.html).
2. **Athlete Personal Details**:
   - Enter Athlete's Full Name, Date of Birth (Age is calculated automatically).
   - Enter Father's Name, Mother's Name, Mobile Number (10 digits), and Email Address.
   - Enter 12-digit Aadhaar Number and Residential Address.
3. **Discipline Selection**:
   - Select your primary skating discipline (*Tenacity / Quad*, *Speed Inline*, *Roller Hockey*, *Inline Freestyle*, *Skateboarding*, etc.).
4. **Document Uploads**:
   - **Passport Photo**: Upload a clear front-facing passport size photo. Use the built-in interactive cropping tool to position and crop your photo.
   - **Aadhaar Proof**: Upload scanned PDF / JPG of Aadhaar Card.
   - **DOB Proof**: Upload scanned Birth Certificate / School ID.
5. **Fee Payment & Submission**:
   - Annual registration fee (e.g., ₹500) is displayed based on discipline.
   - Scan the displayed **UPI QR Code** using any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) or copy the UPI VPA (`rsam@upi`).
   - Enter your **UPI Transaction Reference Number / UTR**.
   - Click **Confirm & Pay** (Base Fee: ₹50.00 + payment gateway charges).

---

### 4. Receiving Your RSAM Registration Number & Certificate

Upon successful registration:
1. **RSAM Registration Number**: You are immediately assigned a unique, concise RSAM Registration Number formatted as `R26yymmdd` + sequence (e.g., `R260908001` for registration on Sept 8, 2026).
2. **Instant WhatsApp Notification**: If WhatsApp Bot is active, a confirmation message containing your `RSAM Reg No: R260908001` and athlete summary is sent to your registered mobile number.
3. **PDF Certificate via Email**: An official **RSAM Athlete Registration Certificate (PDF)** featuring your cropped passport photo, QR verification badge, and registration number is automatically generated and emailed to your address.

---

### 5. Online Certificate Verification

To verify the authenticity of an athlete's registration:
1. Visit the website section **`#certificate`**.
2. Enter the athlete's **RSAM Registration Number** (e.g. `R260908001`) into the search bar.
3. The system verifies the record and displays the official athlete accreditation certificate.

---

### 6. Official RSAM Racing Uniform (Skinsuit)

Under the **Official Recognition** section (`#certificate`), athletes and parents can review the official **RSAM Skater Skinsuit Showcase**. 
- All athletes representing Moradabad district in UPRSA State Championships are required to wear the official RSAM race uniform (displaying the front RSAM emblem and back Moradabad district designation).
