Official Rules & Governance

Terms & Conditions & Athlete Declaration

Guidelines, code of conduct, anti-duplicate rules, and indemnity undertaking for athletes participating in RSAM activities and championships.

1

Registered Discipline Limitation

I am aware that I am permitted to participate ONLY in the registered discipline (as mentioned in the registration application) in any District, State, National, Open Championships, and any violation of this rule will be subject to strict disciplinary action by RSAM.

2

Single Registration & Strict Anti-Duplicate Rule

I understand that multiple registrations are strictly not allowed for the same athlete. Any violation of this rule will result in strict disciplinary action, instant invalidation of both registrations, and total forfeiture of the registration fee.

3

Document Authenticity & Verification

I have attached valid proof of identity, address, and date of birth as per RSAM guidelines and will produce the necessary original documents whenever requested by RSAM or the State Association (UPRSA/RSFI).

4

Communication & Official Notice Authorization

I authorize RSAM to use my contact details (email and mobile number) for sharing any marketing, promotional, upcoming events news, schedule updates, and official communications via SMS, WhatsApp, and Email.

5

Indemnity & Risk Waiver Undertaking

I / my ward agree that RSAM, its members, officials, and organizers shall NOT be held responsible for any injury, accident, or loss of any nature during practice sessions, championships, or journeys, whether on or off the playing area. I also expressly waive the right to claim any damages or compensation from the organizers or association for any such incidents.

6

Direct UPI Payment & UTR Verification Policy

Payments made via Direct UPI QR are subject to validation of the 12-digit UTR transaction number and payment receipt screenshot by RSAM Admin. Submitting false, random, or duplicate UTR numbers will result in immediate rejection of registration and permanent blacklist from future RSAM events.
